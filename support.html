Blu Studio Support

Help for the dealership photo app provided by BLU Productions.

What Blu Studio is

Blu Studio is the app BLU Productions gives to the lot porters, photographers and managers at the car dealerships it works with. It shows which vehicles on your lot still need photos, guides you through shooting each one, and sends the finished photos to your dealership's inventory listings.

It is a tool for dealership staff, not a public app. It only works with a store code from BLU Productions.

Getting access

Ask your store's manager for your dealership's Blu Studio store code (it looks like BLU-XXXX-XXXX). Open the app, type the code once, and the app is set up for your store. You only ever enter it once per phone or computer.

If your manager does not have the code, or your dealership is new to BLU Productions, contact us using the details below and we will set it up.

Installing the app: iPhone users get it through the link BLU Productions sends your manager. Android and desktop downloads are on the Blu Studio download page.

Common problems

The app will not open after an update

On iPhone, open the TestFlight app (if you installed Blu Studio through it) and tap Update next to Blu Studio, then open Blu Studio again. On Android, download the newest version from the download page above and install it over the old one; your store code is kept. If it still will not open, restart the phone and try once more, then contact us.

My store code is not accepted

Check the code character by character: it uses letters and numbers only, with no letter O, letter I, zero or one. Make sure the phone has signal or Wi-Fi, since the code is checked online. If it still fails, the code may have been reissued; ask your manager or contact us for the current one.

Photos are not showing on the vehicle listing

Photos usually reach the listing within a few hours of upload, depending on your dealership's listing provider. Make sure the shoot was finished in the app (tap Finish) and that the phone stayed online until the upload bar completed. If photos are still missing the next day, send us the stock number and we will trace them.

The camera is black or asks for permission

Blu Studio needs camera access to photograph vehicles. On iPhone go to Settings, find Blu Studio, and turn on Camera. On Android go to Settings, Apps, Blu Studio, Permissions, and allow Camera. Then reopen the app.

Something else

Tell us what you were doing, your dealership, and the stock number if one is involved. A screenshot helps.
